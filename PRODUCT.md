# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Couples considering premium wedding photography across the Lake District, UK and Europe. A second audience is prospective clients and employers assessing Luke Brennan’s web development and design work.

## Product Purpose

A fictional wedding-photography brand and working website portfolio piece. Success means the photographs draw visitors in, the site feels professionally art-directed, and visitors can readily explore the work, understand coverage and pricing, and begin an enquiry.

## Operating Context

A responsive static website hosted publicly on GitHub Pages at https://maeveardenphotography.co.uk/. The connected source repository is lukebrennan/Maeve-Arden-Photography. Generated HTML is committed; Python templates generate the pages and compact CSS. There is no frontend framework or required installation to view the site.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the portfolio, visual story pages, experience and collections, about, journal articles, credits, privacy and two-step enquiry demonstration.
- The primary enquiry action is “Check your date.” Collections remain illustrative: The Intimate from £1,450, The Story from £2,850, The Complete from £4,900, and bespoke destination coverage.
- Preserve the functional gallery filters, keyboard-operated image viewer, focus return, mobile navigation, validation, flexible dates, selected-collection links and editable enquiry preview.
- The form does not send information, reserve dates or check actual availability. These limits must stay clear.
- Keep responsive local images, accessibility, semantic HTML, reduced-motion support, SEO metadata, internal links and GitHub project-subpath compatibility.
- Keep the site quick and text restrained. The user rejected oversized typography, a slow experience and a generic AI-looking layout.

## Brand Commitments

Maeve Arden Photography is the confirmed brand name. The identity should be modern, sleek and professional, while demonstrating strong web design and development craft. The user approved the photo-led layout, then explicitly pinned cream and dark grey and asked to restore its elegant, premium feel. Use refined editorial serif display type with compact, legible sans-serif UI; avoid blue surfaces and oversized headlines. A monogram, if used, must clearly read MA rather than AA; the user has now requested a logo. Use the outlined Bodoni Moda MA lockup with the full name and Photography descriptor, its narrow-screen wordmark, and the matching favicon.

## Evidence on Hand

Seventeen verified, credited Unsplash source photographs are bundled in responsive AVIF/WebP formats. Sources and licensing are recorded in IMAGE-SOURCES.md and assets/images/manifest.json. Fourteen photographs are curated into the main portfolio, with explicit home thumbnail choices and a six-image gallery rail. These are visual studies, not commissioned work by a real Maeve Arden. There are no genuine customer testimonials, awards or booking statistics to invent.

## Product Principles

- Let the photographs do the work.
- Make the path from portfolio to pricing to enquiry clear.
- Design should feel authored for this photography, rather than assembled from generic marketing sections.
- Keep the fictional-brand and demo-form disclosures honest and unobtrusive.
- Treat speed, accessibility and mobile quality as part of the design.
