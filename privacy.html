Maeve Arden is a fictional photography brand and this site is a design and development portfolio project by Luke Brennan.

The enquiry preview

The form uses your details in your browser to display an enquiry preview. It does not send them to a server, check a real calendar or store them in cookies or browser storage. The preview exists only in the current page; this website does not save your form details for later visits.

If you choose “Copy enquiry”, the text is copied to your device’s clipboard. You control what you do with that copy.

Images, cookies and analytics

Photographs are served from the same website. This site does not add analytics, advertising, cookies or tracking scripts. The hosting provider may process ordinary connection information, such as IP addresses, to serve and protect the website.

External links

Links to GitHub and Unsplash take you to services with their own privacy policies. You can review those policies on the respective services.
