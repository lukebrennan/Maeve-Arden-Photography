Talk about a destination wedding

Before you enquire.

More about the approach and what happens next.

What if we feel awkward in front of the camera?+

You are in good company. Simple prompts, movement and good light give you something to do without asking you to perform. Portrait time can be short and relaxed.

Can we choose a destination wedding?+

Yes. For the concept, destination coverage is tailored around the celebration, with travel shown separately in a personal quote. Choose “Destination” on the enquiry form to explore that journey.

How much time should we set aside for portraits?+

A short, flexible window is a useful starting point. Discuss the light, locations and any family groups in advance so photographs fit comfortably around the day.

Can we customise a collection?+

The collections are starting points. Additional coverage, albums and a second photographer can be discussed around the plans. The enquiry form lets you share what you have in mind.

Is this a real photography business?+

Maeve Arden is a fictional brand created as a web design and development portfolio project. Images are credited stock photography, prices illustrate the service journey, and the form previews an enquiry without sending it.
