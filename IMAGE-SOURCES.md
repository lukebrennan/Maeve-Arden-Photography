The license excludes selling unmodified images and creating a competing image collection. Attribution is retained here for provenance.

The images are concept stock photography, not work photographed by Maeve Arden. Avoid presenting them as the photographer's own commissioned work.

## Hero and sharing crops

`hero-landscape-{width}.webp` and `.avif` are a separate 5:4 crop of the hero, centered at 50% horizontally and 30% vertically. The crop preserves both faces and removes pixels outside the displayed composition. The full portrait hero variants remain available for portfolio use.

`social.jpg` is a 1200 × 630 progressive JPEG, 137,860 bytes, cropped from the same hero photograph for social previews.

## Rebuilding

With Pillow 12 or later built with WebP and AVIF support, run `python3 scripts/prepare-images.py --download`. This downloads missing source JPEGs into the adjacent workspace `tmp/maeve-photo-sources/` cache and prepares missing local assets, manifest, source notes and contact sheet. Existing encoded files and their embedded provenance are preserved; use --rebuild only when intentionally re-encoding, then restore provenance. Source cache files are deliberately outside the deployable site.
