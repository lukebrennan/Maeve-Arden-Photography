# Grounded redesign directions

The mechanism: move from a small set of documentary photographs to coverage choices and an enquiry while keeping the feeling of the work intact.

Audience scene: couples browsing photography together on a phone or laptop, comparing how a photographer sees people and places, then checking practical coverage.

Cultural home: photographic publications, places they travel to together, thoughtful correspondence, gallery visits, celebrations and personal keepsakes.

The two predictable ruts are excluded: the cream-and-sage wedding template with italic serif headlines, and its all-black fashion-site opposite with oversized type.

1. **The photo essay.** A documentary publication’s deliberate image sequence: generous photographs, succinct captions and sequential exploration. Strong photographic clarity; familiar art-book territory.
2. **The private view.** A contemporary exhibition’s hanging plan: unequal image scales, precise labels, space between encounters and an index that makes exploration tactile. A spatial rather than marketing composition.
3. **The personal invitation.** Distinctive correspondence: a committed ink-colour field, a photographic reveal, generous personal names and a direct invitation to start a conversation. Can carry contact and journal pages without selling through cards.
4. **The folded atlas.** A destination folio’s unfolding sequence: landscapes lead, portraits interrupt the scale, a narrow place-and-story index accompanies the images, and practical details stay in a consistent margin. Brings the Lake District, London and destination promise into the visitor’s exploration. No fake geography or literal map chrome.
5. **The celebration programme.** A live event’s programme: image-led opening, an ordered account of what happens before/during/after, and coverage shown as clear choices within that story. Strong service clarity, less photographic freedom.
6. **The record sleeve.** Personal music and memory: a decisive cover photograph, a human wordmark, liner-note-like story details and a physical-feeling next/previous rhythm. Celebratory without inventing music features.
7. **The viewing folio.** The way a carefully chosen place is introduced: one expansive scene, close material/people studies, short contextual notes and an invitation to talk. A hospitality/documentary system, with the risk of giving place too much weight over people.

The user confirmed the core product brief and explicitly chose building directly, followed by review. The current session is code-led; generated page comps and a mockup-selection round are not the chosen workflow.

## Challenger assessment and commitment

Assigned candidate 4, Folded atlas, is retained. It connects the real Lake District/London/destination content with a clear photographic browsing mechanism.

- Dark-first developer console: declines audience resonance and product clarity. Transfer: obvious selected/control states; refuse its panels and syntax costume.
- Wuxia painted hoarding: declines both axes because painted spectacle substitutes for the documentary work. Transfer: let one held photographic moment dominate; refuse brush titles and cinematic billing.
- Exposure record zone sheets: competitive on photographic craft/audience, weaker on couples' product clarity because it describes an editing workbench. Board and hero inspected. Transfer: precise image boundaries and legible practical margins; refuse zone ramps, hatchwork and condensed technical lettering.
- Teletext service: declines both axes because the grid degrades the work and becomes the experience. Transfer: concise, consistent navigation; refuse bitmap numerals and broadcast palette.
- Luminescent alien understory: declines both axes because glow and branching obscure the portfolio. Transfer: orchestrate attention at one moment only; refuse ambient animation.
- Chromatophore skin: declines both axes because distorting pixels contradicts honest photographs. Transfer: meaningful interaction feedback only; refuse synthetic photo transformations.
